EXPLICIT JUDGEMENT CRITERIA:
Evaluate how Novel (unusual/significant), New (breaking/status quo change), and Local (proximity to Greater Glasgow) the story is.

SCORING RUBRIC:
- 8.0 to 10.0 (HIGH): Major shifts in the status quo. Highly novel AND highly local. Examples: Major council policy shifts, significant infrastructure project updates, major public scandals, or severe regional emergencies.
- 5.0 to 7.9 (MEDIUM): Important but standard news. Has some local impact or moderate novelty. Examples: Routine but large-scale local council updates, medium-scale regional developments, or notable public events.
- 1.0 to 4.9 (LOW): Niche or hyper-localised news. Lacks novelty or broad impact. Examples: Minor neighborhood disputes, individual petty crimes, or very minor administrative updates.
- 0.0 (EXCLUDE): Charity fundraising, product/business reviews, generic "feel good" fluff, clickbait, OR transient alerts (e.g., past weather warnings) that have already expired.
