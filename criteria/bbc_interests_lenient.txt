BROAD DOMAINS FOR APPROVAL:

1. PHYSICS, ASTRONOMY, EARTH SCIENCE & SPACE EXPLORATION
   INCLUDE: Any articles about physics, space exploration, astronomy, earth science, stargazing, eclipses, or astrophysics.

2. HISTORY
   INCLUDE: Any articles about archaeology or anthropology.

3. RUNNING & OUTDOORS
   INCLUDE: Any articles directly and explicitly about road running, track running, fell running, or trail running.
   REJECT: Articles about cricket, baseball, or any other sport where "runs" are merely a method of scoring.

4. OUTDOORS EXPLORATION
   INCLUDE: Any articles about hiking, mountaineering, wild camping, caving, or outdoor exploration.

5. TECHNOLOGY & AI
   INCLUDE: Any articles about Generative AI, LLMs, smart home technology, wearable technology or the "quantified self" movement.

6. SCIENCE FICTION
   INCLUDE: Articles about science fiction franchises (e.g., Star Wars, Star Trek, Stargate, Babylon 5, Sliders) or disaster movies.

7. EDUCATION
   INCLUDE: Articles about independent secondary schools in Scotland.

8. NATURAL DISASTERS
   INCLUDE: Articles reporting on a major natural disaster (e.g., earthquake, tsunami, hurricane, volcanic eruption) where the main reported event occurred WITHIN THE LAST 24 HOURS.
   REJECT: Any disaster coverage where the main reported event occurred MORE THAN 24 HOURS AGO.
   REJECT: Articles reporting on man-made disasters such as wars, terrorist attacks or military strikes. 
   REJECT: Articles reporting on famines or droughts.

   HOW TO DETERMINE EVENT AGE:
   - Check the text for temporal markers relative to the 'Published' timestamp (e.g., references like "yesterday", "two days ago", "earlier this week", "following [past day]'s quake", or a date >24h prior).
   - If the main disaster event happened over 24 hours ago, you MUST REJECT the article, regardless of whether it discusses ongoing rescue efforts, rising death tolls, or official statements.
MACRO-EXCLUSIONS (Always Reject):
REJECT: Celebrity gossip or reality television.

REJECT: High-brow art and literature.
REJECT: Reject articles would only be of interest to the residents of a single specific town, county, or region (e.g., a local road closure in Cornwall, a localized council dispute in Yorkshire, or tips on how to spot a rare bird in Newcastle). 
REJECT: Articles containing general weather forecasts UNLESS they are primarily about the metrological mechanism (e.g. Jet Stream) driving that forecast.
REJECT: Natural Disaster Follow-ups. You MUST reject articles detailing the humanitarian aftermath (e.g., rescue operations, casualty updates, rebuilding) of natural disasters. ONLY approve if the text contains explicit temporal evidence that the initial disaster struck WITHIN THE LAST 24 HOURS (e.g., "hit this morning," "struck today," "has just occurred"). If an article discusses established rescue efforts or a high death toll but lacks a clear timestamp proving the disaster started today, you must assume it is an older follow-up story and REJECT it.
