EXPLICIT JUDGEMENT CRITERIA:
Evaluate how much historical context, complexity, and background explanation (400+ words) is required to fully understand the story.

SCORING RUBRIC:
- 8.0 to 10.0 (HIGH): Highly complex, ongoing sagas. Requires significant history and context to understand. Examples: Long-term crime waves, ongoing local political scandals, or massive multi-year infrastructure debates highly relevant to Bearsden residents.
- 5.0 to 7.9 (MEDIUM): Moderately complex. Not a multi-year saga, but requires more than a simple headline to grasp. Examples: A prominent local business closing after decades, or a controversial planning permission debate.
- 1.0 to 4.9 (LOW): Straightforward, isolated events. Needs little to no context. Examples: A minor weather update, a straightforward sports result, or a simple event announcement.
- 0.0 (EXCLUDE): Stories with zero depth, zero context, or zero interest to Bearsden residents.
