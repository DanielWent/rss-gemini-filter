GEO CATEGORIES:
- GEO CATEGORY 1 (Immediate Local & Jurisdictional): Stories situated in Bearsden, Westerton, Blairdardie, Anniesland, Netherton, Temple, Dawsholm, OR council-wide policies affecting East Dunbartonshire as a whole.
- GEO CATEGORY 2 (Regional Hubs & Borders): Stories situated in major commercial centres within 15-20 mins drive of Bearsden (Milngavie Town Centre, Glasgow West End, Braehead, Glasgow City Centre, Clydebank, Mugdock Country Park), OR council-wide policies affecting Glasgow City as a whole.
- GEO CATEGORY 3 (Outside Actionable Area): Stories situated anywhere else (e.g., Kirkintilloch, East/South Glasgow, generic UK-wide news, or Scotland-wide updates).

EXPLICIT JUDGEMENT CRITERIA:
Evaluate the practical, actionable impact this news has on a family living in Westerton, Bearsden. Prioritize proximity and immediate disruption or benefit over novelty.

SCORING RUBRIC:
- 8.0 to 10.0 (HIGH - Direct & Immediate Impact): High proximity (Geo Category 1) with direct consequences on daily life or local mobility.
    - CRIME: Only score property crimes (break-ins, car thefts) or major incidents situated specifically in Bearsden, Westerton, or Anniesland. Strictly ignore petty crimes in wider East Dunbartonshire.
    - ROAD CLOSURES: Any road closure situated in Geo Category 1.
    - RAIL DISRUPTION: Disruption that directly impacts train services through Westerton Station specifically.
- 5.0 to 7.9 (MEDIUM - Hyper-Local Amenities & Regional Disruptions):
    - ROAD CLOSURES: Significant multi-day road closures in Geo Category 2 likely to cause severe delays or require significant diversions.
    - HYPER-LOCAL LEISURE (Geo Category 1 ONLY): New coffee shops, restaurants, business openings, or upgrades to local play parks/primary-aged amenities located strictly in Bearsden, Westerton, Anniesland.
    - MAJOR REGIONAL LEISURE (Geo Category 2): Updates on significant civic exhibitions or major infrastructure developments.
- 1.0 to 4.9 (LOW - Minimal Practical Value & Geo 2 Hospitality):
    - TRANSPORT DISRUPTIONS: All other road closures (e.g., minor roadworks in Geo Category 2), any rail disruption that does NOT directly impact Westerton Station, and ALL bus disruptions (bus news must always score low).
    - OTHER: Local stories requiring zero immediate action, temporary traffic lights with minimal delay, or routine business/hospitality openings situated in Geo Category 2 (Glasgow City Centre/West End).
- 0.0 (EXCLUDE): Generic national financial advice, changes to nursery/early-years funding, short-term transient alerts that have expired, OR Geo Category 3 stories (UNLESS they are about tourist/nature day trips).
