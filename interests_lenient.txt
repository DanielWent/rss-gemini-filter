BROAD DOMAINS FOR APPROVAL:

1. PHYSICS, ASTRONOMY, EARTH SCIENCE & SPACE EXPLORATION
   - Any articles related to physics, space exploration, astronomy, earth science, stargazing, eclipses, or astrophysics.

2. HISTORY
   - Any articles related to archaeology, anthropology or earth sciences.

3. ENDURANCE SPORTS & OUTDOORS
   - Any articles related to running, sprinting, marathons, trail running, hiking, mountaineering, wild camping, caving, or outdoor exploration.

4. TECHNOLOGY & AI
   - Any articles related to Generative AI, LLMs, smart home technology, wearable technology or the "quantified self" movement.

5. SCIENCE FICTION
   - Articles related to science fiction franchises (e.g., Star Wars, Star Trek, Stargate, Babylon 5, Sliders) or disaster movies.

6. EDUCATION
   - Articles relating specifically to independent secondary schools in Scotland.

7. NATURAL DISASTERS
   - The very first, initial reports of a new natural disaster.   
   - Developments related to existing natural disasters that indicate a significant change to the scale or urgency of the developing situation.

MACRO-EXCLUSIONS (Reject these):
- Pure politics (e.g., partisan debates, election campaigning, or unpassed bills), geopolitics, war, or military operations.
- Celebrity gossip or reality television.
- Competitive team sports (football, rugby, etc.).
- High-brow art and literature.
- Stories about existing and ongoing natural disasters that do not indicate a significant change to the scale or urgency of the situation.
