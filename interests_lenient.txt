BROAD DOMAINS FOR APPROVAL:

1. PHYSICS, ASTRONOMY, EARTH SCIENCE & SPACE EXPLORATION
   INCLUDE: Any articles related to physics, space exploration, astronomy, earth science, stargazing, eclipses, or astrophysics.

2. HISTORY
   INCLUDE: Any articles related to archaeology or anthropology.

3. RUNNING & OUTDOORS
   INCLUDE: Any articles related to road running, sprinting, fell running, or trail running.
   REJECT: Articles about cricket, baseball, or any other sport where "runs" are merely a method of scoring.

4. OUTDOORS EXPLORATION
   INCLUDE: Any articles related to hiking, mountaineering, wild camping, caving, or outdoor exploration.

5. TECHNOLOGY & AI
   INCLUDE: Any articles related to Generative AI, LLMs, smart home technology, wearable technology or the "quantified self" movement.

6. SCIENCE FICTION
   INCLUDE: Articles related to science fiction franchises (e.g., Star Wars, Star Trek, Stargate, Babylon 5, Sliders) or disaster movies.

7. EDUCATION
   INCLUDE: Articles relating specifically to independent secondary schools in Scotland.

8. NATURAL DISASTERS
   INCLUDE: Articles reporting the immediate occurrence of a new major natural disaster (e.g., earthquakes, tsunamis, hurricanes).
   REJECT: "Day two" or aftermath coverage. Reject articles focusing on rescue operations, incremental casualty updates, survivor accounts, humanitarian aid, or economic rebuilding UNLESS the article is primarily reporting on the initial, breaking occurrence of the disaster itself.

MACRO-EXCLUSIONS (Always Reject):
REJECT: Celebrity gossip or reality television.
REJECT: General coverage of "Athletics" championships or multi-discipline sports events (e.g., overall tournament news, stadium attendance, or medal tables) UNLESS the article is a dedicated race report focusing exclusively on a specific running, sprinting, or marathon event.
REJECT: High-brow art and literature.
REJECT: Reject articles would only be of interest to the residents of a single specific town, county, or region (e.g., a local road closure in Cornwall, a localized council dispute in Yorkshire, or tips on how to spot a rare bird in Newcastle). 
REJECT: Articles containing general weather forecasts UNLESS they are primarily about the metrological mechanism (e.g. Jet Stream) driving that forecast.
