BROAD DOMAINS FOR APPROVAL:

1. PHYSICS, ASTRONOMY, EARTH SCIENCE & SPACE EXPLORATION
INCLUDE: Any articles related to physics, space exploration, astronomy, earth science, stargazing, eclipses, or astrophysics.

2. HISTORY
INCLUDE: Any articles related to archaeology or anthropology.

3. RUNNING & OUTDOORS
INCLUDE: Any articles related to running, sprinting, marathons, trail running, 

4. OUTDOORS EXPLORATION
INCLUDE: Any articles related to hiking, mountaineering, wild camping, caving, or outdoor exploration.

5. TECHNOLOGY & AI
INCLUDE: Any articles related to Generative AI, LLMs, smart home technology, wearable technology or the "quantified self" movement.

6. SCIENCE FICTION
INCLUDE: Articles related to science fiction franchises (e.g., Star Wars, Star Trek, Stargate, Babylon 5, Sliders) or disaster movies.

7. EDUCATION
INCLUDE: Articles relating specifically to independent secondary schools in Scotland.

8. NATURAL DISASTERS
INCLUDE: The very first, initial reports of a new natural disaster.   
INCLUDE: Developments related to existing natural disasters that indicate a significant change to the scale or urgency of the developing situation.
REJECT: Stories about existing and ongoing natural disasters that do not indicate a significant change to the scale or urgency of the situation.

ALWAYS REJECT: Articles that are clearly written for the explicit attention of people living in one specific regional area outside of Scotland. For example, reject articles discussing the best way to view an eclipse from Yorkshire, London, Cornwall, or the Isle of Wight.
