BROAD DOMAINS FOR APPROVAL:

1. PURE PHYSICS, ASTRONOMY & SPACE
   - Any news related to pure physics, space exploration, astronomy, stargazing, eclipses, or astrophysics.

2. EARTH SCIENCE & NATURAL HISTORY
   - Any news regarding geology, archaeology, paleontology or earth sciences.

3. ENDURANCE SPORTS & OUTDOORS
   - Any articles about running, marathons, trail running, hiking, mountaineering, wild camping, or outdoor exploration.

4. TECHNOLOGY & AI
   - General news regarding Generative AI, LLMs, and smart home technology.

5. Personal Interest
   - News regarding classic science fiction franchises (e.g., Star Trek, Stargate, Sliders) or disaster movies.
   - News affecting independent secondary education in Scotland.

6. Natural Disasters
   - Significant developments related to major natural disasters that completely change the scale and urgency of the situation.

MACRO-EXCLUSIONS (Reject these):
- Pure politics, geopolitics, war, or military operations.
- Celebrity gossip or reality television.
- Competitive team sports (football, rugby, etc.).
- High-brow art and literature.
