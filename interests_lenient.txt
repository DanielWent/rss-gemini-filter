BROAD DOMAINS FOR APPROVAL:

1. PURE PHYSICS, ASTRONOMY & SPACE
   - Any articles discussing or related to physics, space exploration, astronomy, stargazing, solar eclipses, lunar eclipses, or astrophysics.

2. EARTH SCIENCE & NATURAL HISTORY
   - Any articles discussing or related to geology, archaeology, paleontology or earth sciences.

3. ENDURANCE SPORTS & OUTDOORS
   - Any articles about running, marathons, trail running, hiking, mountaineering, wild camping, or outdoor exploration.

4. TECHNOLOGY & AI
   - Any articles related to Generative AI, LLMs, and smart home technology.

5. Personal Interest
   - Articles regarding classic science fiction franchises (e.g., Star Trek, Stargate, Sliders) or disaster movies.
   - Articles relating to independent secondary schools in Scotland.

6. Natural Disasters
   - The very first, initial reports of a major natural disaster.   
   - Developments related to existing natural disasters that indicate a significant change to the scale or urgency of the situation.

7. DIRECT DEMOGRAPHIC IMPACT (SCOTLAND)
   - News regarding finalised policies, enacted legislation, or immediate financial changes directly and tangibly impacting a family that: lives in Scotland, has a mortgage, owns an electric vehicle (EV), and has two primary-school-aged children.
   - Examples: Enacted laws regarding Scottish primary schools or childcare, confirmed Bank of England interest rate changes affecting mortgages, or new taxation/infrastructure regulations for EVs in Scotland.
   - STRICT CATEGORY EXCLUSIONS: You must reject articles about mere proposals, consultations, political debates, or any legislation/laws that only apply to England, Wales, or Northern Ireland.

MACRO-EXCLUSIONS (Reject these):
- Pure politics (e.g., partisan debates, election campaigning, or unpassed bills), geopolitics, war, or military operations.
- Celebrity gossip or reality television.
- Competitive team sports (football, rugby, etc.).
- High-brow art and literature.
- Stories about existing and ongoing natural disasters that do not indicate a significant change to the scale or urgency of the situation.
